Google Drive access

YDL checks whether a link can be opened without signing in. For restricted links, YDL offers Google sign-in and requests read-only access. This lets it list and download files within the folders you share with the app. YDL never changes files in Google Drive and does not use Google APIs to create, process, or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery, including AI-generated imagery.
